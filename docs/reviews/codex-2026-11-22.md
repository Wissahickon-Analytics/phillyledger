# Adversarial review of the phillyledger plan

## Verdict

The January article and positions dataset are achievable from a pinned extract, but I would not make their publication depend on finishing steps 1 to 6. On a real FY2026 Q4 positions table, all 291 arithmetic checks still passed after I swapped General and Other Funds, and again after I assigned two departments each other’s figures. I would keep the three-package design, move source-page verification ahead of library construction, and publish the new parser’s output only after a cell-by-cell comparison with the launch extract. [Positions checks](DESIGN-FULL.md#L2446) · [January launch option](PLAN.md#L1724)

## Top problems

1. **A correct number can publish under the wrong name with `proof=proven`.** FY2026 Q4 Aviation prints 0 General Fund positions, 845 Other Funds, and 845 total. Swapping the two fund bindings throughout the table gives readers 845 General Fund positions. Swapping Aviation’s key with Board of Ethics gives Aviation 13 total positions. Both changes preserve every declared P-1 identity, including the department and citywide sums. A word box still leads to a real number. **Fix and cost.** Test the binding separately with page crops that show the number, row label, fund caption and spanning year header together. Compare every released cell by edition, row and named column against the pinned baseline, and adjudicate differences on the page. This catches semantic swaps at the cost of reviewing fixtures and differences. [Binding rules](DESIGN-FULL.md#L1640) · [Proof definition](DESIGN-FULL.md#L3484)

2. **The launch corpus is larger and harder than the acceptance test.** The plan expects 19 quarters. The old continuity test explicitly ends at FY2026 Q3. The working checkout has all **20** quarters from FY2022 Q1 through FY2026 Q4, including a hash-verified whole Q4 report. Six sections start with a narrative page headed `TABLE P-1`. In the Q4 whole report, physical page 32 is commentary and a chart, and pages 33 and 34 are the grid. The proposed parser selects the run by `TABLE P-1`, chooses the era from its first page, and requires every page to bind the same columns. Separately, the proposed word adapter returned broken font codes in labels and digits on **20 grid pages in 10 editions**. **Fix and cost.** Inventory the 20 quarters by source hash, distinguish a section cover from a grid by its captions, and test font decoding on real pages before building the rest of the kit. The old repair removed the placeholders on all 20 affected pages in my probe. Validating the resulting digits against the image is the remaining cost. [Build criterion](PLAN.md#L1592) · [First-page binding](DESIGN-FULL.md#L1655) · [Old continuity set](../gov-budget-data/packages/gov-budget-etl/tests/test_qcmr_positions_continuity.py#L19) · [Q4 source receipt](../gov-budget-data/data/phila/qcmr/positions/FY26_Q4.source.json#L3)

3. **The vocabulary passes a formula that the source explicitly contradicts.** Legacy cash `under_over` is described as `budget − total`. The reviewed identity is `budget = total + accrued + under_over`. Whenever accrued is nonzero, the published explanation gives the wrong answer. `Derived` requires exactly two operands for a difference, so the correct description cannot be entered as written. **Fix and cost.** Support the signed operands this table actually uses, or withhold the derivation until it can be expressed. Test it on a printed row with nonzero accruals. This buys a truthful manifest for the cost of one vocabulary change or one temporarily unavailable description. [Column reference](vocab/REFERENCE.md#L55) · [Operand restriction](vocab/measures.py#L57) · [Reviewed source identity](../gov-budget-data/config/layouts/qcmr-cash.yml#L495)

4. **An acknowledgment can survive a different error.** Matching pins the document, check, table instance, total row, column, residual and total page. A total of 100 against parts 40 and 61 has the same residual as 100 against 41 and 60. The acknowledgment still matches. For `MISSING_TOTAL`, the residual is `None`, so even a change in the sum of the contributors can go undetected. A cell participating in both a pass and an acknowledged failure also satisfies two published proof definitions. **Fix and cost.** Pin the total and the sorted signed operand keys and printed values, including null states. Keep coordinates out of that fingerprint so harmless movement stays harmless. Publish arithmetic participation and acknowledged exceptions independently, or specify their precedence. This buys re-review when the evidence changes and costs additional review of genuine changes. [Expected failure fields](DESIGN-FULL.md#L956) · [Matcher](DESIGN-FULL.md#L3524) · [Overlapping proof definitions](DESIGN-FULL.md#L3490)

5. **Coverage can pass after evidence disappears.** If one required operand vanishes, the result is `MISSING_OPERAND`. Even a check whose results are *all* missing produces only a warning. Other cells can retain `proven` through a different sum. At the page level, the supplemental parser explicitly fails when it finds zero statements. If it loses one fund and finds the others, that test still passes. The coverage ledger likewise says an untitled BIRT page falls outside every run. **Fix and cost.** For P-1, declare expected quarters, grid pages, required rows, printed columns and applicable check instances. Record legitimate blanks explicitly. For open families, add expected statement instances and reasons for excluded pages. This detects omissions that arithmetic cannot see. It costs a small, independently reviewed inventory per layout. [Missing operands](DESIGN-FULL.md#L3464) · [Supplemental parser](DESIGN-FULL.md#L2632) · [BIRT coverage entry](coverage.tsv#L64)

6. **A slower release can undo a newer one.** The executor verifies staged files, uploads public files, then calls ordinary `put` on `latest.json`. Applying create-only to that pointer makes the second release fail. Allowing unconditional replacement lets two runs plan from the same predecessor and the slower one replace the winner. The old repo already handles both cases correctly. **Fix and cost.** Create immutable objects conditionally, verify existing bytes on a conflict, and advance the pointer only against the ETag read with the predecessor. A losing publisher must reread and replan. This buys safe retries and costs a conflict path and a two-writer test. [New release executor](DESIGN-FULL.md#L2269) · [Old immutable and pointer writes](../gov-budget-data/packages/gov-budget-etl/src/gov_budget_etl/release_s3.py#L513) · [Cloudflare conditional operations](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/api/s3/conditional-operations/)

7. **A replaced PDF can be invisible to `watch`.** The full-design watcher fetches listings and Wayback results. It never observes current PDF bytes. If the City replaces an attachment at the same URL and leaves the listing unchanged, `plan_watch` sees exactly the inputs it saw yesterday. The engineering plan expressly calls for refetching current editions to catch this case. **Fix and cost.** Have the watch workflow periodically fetch and hash the current attachments it promises to monitor, then put the change in its PR. This buys detection of silent replacements for bandwidth and some run time. Parsing and publishing can still wait for review. [Watcher executor](DESIGN-FULL.md#L2163) · [Required byte recheck](PLAN.md#L898)

## What is right

Keep the whole-PDF archive, physical page numbers, typed blanks and dashes, exact count arithmetic, printed labels beside keys, and the rule that arithmetic cannot supply replacement digits. Keep the edition-level block on arithmetic failures, cross-edition warnings, explicit `--spend`, and FY2008 Q2 withholding. Keep the separation between Philadelphia facts and generic extraction. These choices preserve evidence and let one bad edition stop without holding every other edition hostage. ## Answers to questions 1 to 13

**1. Size.** Too large as an up-front review commitment. The 106 module estimates do add to 14,220. They exclude the row schemas, entity aliases, review records and tests, precisely where a change can assign the right figure to the wrong department. Even an illustrative 15-minute first look per module is 26½ hours, or roughly 9 to 27 weeks of Nick’s review time before substantive review. Keep the long-term package boundaries and make each family earn its abstractions. The cut table gives the savings and risks. [Module totals and exclusions](DESIGN-FULL.md#L3949)

**2. First release.** The shortest safe path is already in the plan as **Option A**. Pin the old release, inventory the 20 source documents, check every figure used in the article against its printed row and caption, and publish a versioned extract with its coverage, hashes and source pages. If the pinned release ends at Q3, verify Q4 separately or state the earlier cutoff prominently. For a release claiming all 20 quarters are checked, reconcile all 20; checking article figures alone warrants a narrower claim. If the new parser must supply the download, start with one damaged-font edition and one narrative-cover edition, then run the corpus. The general lexer for unrelated formats, full shape suite, parse-result cache, migration tool and reader can wait. The step-6 canary itself calls `watch`, scheduled for step 13. A pinned-input canary gives January end-to-end coverage without that dependency. [January recommendation](PLAN.md#L1730) · [Canary dependency](DESIGN-FULL.md#L3817) · [Build order](PLAN.md#L1590)

**3. Functional core and imperative shell.** Keep pure parsing, identity resolution, check expansion, acknowledgment matching, release selection and paid-OCR planning. Those decisions benefit from replay. For a cached file read, `LoadRequest → LoadPlan → downloads → reopen` adds little. For parse caching, an inventory can go stale between `keys()` and `get()`. A shell that reads by hash and recomputes a miss is easier to retry. This gives up a printable cache-hit plan and saves types and failure handling. The principles explicitly allow this simplification. [Reader and cache executor](DESIGN-FULL.md#L2312) · [Where the principle should bend](PRINCIPLES.md#L96)

The tooling is viable in a uv workspace **as guardrails**. Install all three importable members in the lint environment, for example with `uv sync --locked --all-packages`. The design already sets `root_packages` and `include_external_packages=true`. Grimp explicitly includes top-level standard-library imports under that option, resolving the document’s question about `os`. Enable Ruff `TID251` and scope the bans to core code. I confirmed that it flagged an aliased `datetime.now()` and `tomllib.load()` but missed a clock call through a local class variable. `pytest --disable-socket` covers Python socket access during tests; the plugin documents a fixture-order limitation and provides no file or clock guard. Keep the planted violations and add one test that exercises each installed package. The cost is a small CI contract, with no claim that passing it proves purity. [Proposed configuration](DESIGN-FULL.md#L3999) · [uv CLI](https://docs.astral.sh/uv/reference/cli/#uv-sync--all-packages) · [Import Linter](https://import-linter.readthedocs.io/en/stable/contract_types/forbidden/) · [Grimp](https://grimp.readthedocs.io/en/stable/usage.html) · [Ruff](https://docs.astral.sh/ruff/rules/banned-api/) · [pytest-socket](https://github.com/miketheman/pytest-socket)

**4. Kit boundary.** A generic words, cells, evidence and arithmetic package is a credible PyPI candidate after step 14. Keep Philadelphia’s vocabulary, identity decisions, review policy and promotion workflow in `datadesk`. Freeze the small interface an outside caller needs. A nine-field `RowRule` already passes a four-parameter *function* test, and choosing an enum or options object can satisfy the no-`bool` rule without simplifying behavior. The 3,660-line estimate leaves just 140 lines beneath the 3,800 cap. The kit’s own proposed test even imports desk-owned vocabulary policy. Make field counts and size review signals; keep hard tests for lost words, ambiguous bindings, provenance and check coverage. This costs discretion in API review and removes an incentive to hide logic in a family helper. [RowRule](DESIGN-FULL.md#L601) · [Shape and budget tests](DESIGN-FULL.md#L3754) · [Desk policy in kit tests](DESIGN-FULL.md#L3798)

**5. Can a wrong number publish?** Yes. The path is page selection → caption and lane binding → row key → lexed amount with word references → arithmetic result → edition gate → scaled release row. The last two steps inherit the interpretation made at the first three. [P-1 parser](DESIGN-FULL.md#L2424) · [Release-row fields](DESIGN-FULL.md#L1535)

| Route | What reaches readers |
|---|---|
| Wrong caption, `proven` | The demonstrated Aviation fund swap preserves all 291 identities. A bad fund-to-column declaration after correct geometric binding has the same effect. |
| Wrong key, `proven` | The demonstrated Aviation and Board of Ethics swap preserves both the key set and the totals. A gate looking for disappearing keys will miss it. |
| Wrong page range, `proven` | A declared range bypasses header search. An internally consistent copy of the same layout for another period or fund can satisfy the schema and sums. This is a design counterexample. I observed the cover-page failure, not a historical wrong-range publication. Verify an independent period or fund anchor and the boundary rows. [Declared-range rule](DESIGN-FULL.md#L1123) |
| Wrong digit, `ocr_arithmetic` | FY2007 Q4 cash OCR read December wage receipts as 65.8 million where the reviewed source reads 85.8 million. Its other errors improved agreement. Even one erroneous tenth of a million can fit the 0.65-million bound for a twelve-month sum. [Reviewed evidence](../gov-budget-data/docs/value-contract-vertical-slice-acceptance.md#L22) · [Rounding rule](dossiers/QCASH.md#L141) |
| Changed operands, `acknowledged` | The equal-residual example in problem 4 passes the specified matcher. The public row should identify the source exception even when the cell also participates in a passing check. [Matching rule](DESIGN-FULL.md#L3524) |

**6. Column vocabulary.** The six lists are a good reviewed vocabulary for the tables tried so far. The tests establish internal consistency on declared examples. They do not establish that the descriptions fit the printed tables. I found these specific defects beyond the legacy cash formula:

- **Monthly opening and closing cash balances** inherit `FLOW` in the runnable code. Only the thirteenth column gets opening and closing level overrides. Summing monthly closing balances therefore looks legitimate to a consumer. Give the monthly balance rows point dates too, with opening dated at the appropriate boundary. This costs row-specific declarations and avoids a meaningless annual sum. [Reference](vocab/REFERENCE.md#L73) · [Runnable declarations](vocab/tables.py#L196) · [Balance chain](dossiers/QCASH.md#L160)
- **O-3 full-time positions** inherit the default `USD` and `FLOW` in both runnable era declarations, with zero row overrides. The full design has a place for a row unit, but the vocabulary test never exercises an effective positions-row measure. Test an actual position row and an adjacent Class 100 dollar row through release encoding. That costs two fixtures and prevents a count shipping as dollars. [Declarations](vocab/tables.py#L108) · [Default unit](vocab/tables.py#L21) · [Dossier units](dossiers/QOBL.md#L210)
- **QSMR OF-1 has a verified reversed sign and an extra column.** The FY2023 Q4 reference says amended minus projection and labels adopted budget present in all layouts. The District prints amended budget, projection, variance and two prior actuals. Local Tax Revenue goes from $1,502,781,017 amended to $1,531,107,781 projected and prints **positive $28,326,764**. One signed example and a count of printed captions would catch both mistakes. [Reference](vocab/REFERENCE.md#L371) · [District report, Table OF-1](https://www.philasd.org/budget/wp-content/uploads/sites/96/2023/08/FY23-Q4-Report_QSMR-w_ADA.pdf)
- **City O-2 has a missing layout.** FY2026 Q4 prints six value columns, omitting the three YTD columns that the sole `nine` era requires. A correct extractor would block this edition under the proposed contract. Add the six-column fixture and bind the year-end form separately. [Proposed eras](DESIGN-FULL.md#L3203) · [Full-report section index](../gov-budget-data/artifacts/source_audits/2026-09-11/pdf_inspection.json#L10)

`Subject` is appropriate for finding candidate comparisons. The June 30 O-2 YTD and following edition’s prior actual deliberately compare equal. Canonical selection separately includes `window`, so that particular cross-window match creates a warning comparison and does **not** automatically replace one canonical series with the other. Within one window, however, `Subject` has no audit status or general accounting-basis field. Collections explicitly tags the pre-accrual column as cash and the accrual component as accruals, but leaves the final FYTD quantity unspecified. Match on compatible accounting basis, unit and reporting scope, retain vintage and audit status, and keep the owner’s cross-edition warnings. **7. Row identity.** The first launch seam is Streets. The exact same ink means Streets plus Sanitation in FY2018–FY2025 and the separate department again in FY2026. A wrong effective year yields a valid, unique key and perfect arithmetic. Code 66 also changed department ownership twice. A merger needs dated predecessor relationships; an undated spelling alias cannot say whether a historical total has the same scope. Keep printed names and edition-scoped OCR aliases. Add paired fixtures on both sides of each launch seam and require review of an entity change even when the row key survives. This costs a short historical crosswalk and protects the trend an article is likely to use. For CF-3, my recommendation on the open decision is to retain printed vendor numbers when present and use edition-local occurrences when absent. Two equal names sorted by payment can trade places without changing their count. Cross-edition vendor identity should remain unresolved in that case. [Streets in old registry](../gov-budget-data/config/entities.yml#L1545) · [Dated reorganizations](dossiers/ENT.md#L110) · [Vendor evidence](dossiers/QSMR.md#L83)

**8. Archive and storage.** **Yes, R2 supports S3 `PutObject` with `If-None-Match: *`.** Cloudflare documents atomic evaluation. For concurrent writes to an absent key, at most one succeeds while its object exists; competing preconditions fail with `412`. For multipart uploads the condition belongs on `CompleteMultipartUpload`. On a `412`, verify the existing bytes against the intended SHA-256. Use `If-Match` on the observed ETag for a pointer update. Git and R2 are separate commits: a failed index PR can leave an unindexed blob, and concurrent PRs can disagree about which version is current. Revalidate the index on merge and make a retry reuse a verified blob. This costs a reconciliation check. Two OCR jobs can also both incur the charge before either tries its create-only write; use the build lock already proposed. [R2 conditional operations](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/api/s3/conditional-operations/) · [Old publisher](../gov-budget-data/packages/gov-budget-etl/src/gov_budget_etl/release_s3.py#L524) · [OCR build lock](PLAN.md#L1089)

Nick has accepted the public-archive risk. The City’s terms allow limited single-page sharing and require written permission for broader republication. I cannot determine their application to each report. There is also an operational consequence: the plan puts PDFs and public datasets in the same bucket, so turning off public access to that bucket can break both. Preserve the official URL and hash in each citation, and test withdrawing public access to an individual PDF while keeping datasets and correction notices available. That buys a workable response at the cost of one access-control test. [Archive layout and accepted decision](PLAN.md#L935) · [City terms](https://www.phila.gov/terms-of-use/) · [R2 public buckets](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/buckets/public-buckets/)

**9. Release and reader.** Defer the reader implementation. Ship versioned CSV and Parquet links, a hashed manifest, column definitions, proof meanings, a citation example, and a checksum command. Read both packaged formats back and compare their typed contents against the approved rows. Identical bytes across two builds merely reproduce a consistent encoding error. Later, settle the reader’s public return type: the engineering plan promises pandas by default, while the design example returns an Arrow table. Waiting costs a convenience API and gives the first real users a chance to inform that choice. [Reader promise](PLAN.md#L1346) · [Reader example](DESIGN-FULL.md#L2312) · [Packaged-byte check](PLAN.md#L1072)

**10. OCR.** Keep the confidence floor of 95 and the distinct `ocr_arithmetic` label. Add a source-page review sample for each scan layout, including high-confidence numbers, signs, decimals, labels and blanks; inspect every article figure. Where a second source exists, compare missing and extra tokens as well as overlapping numeric tokens. The specified comparison misses an amount dropped entirely by one source, a documented School collections failure. If the primary source has a wrong digit, the reading rule correctly refuses a substitution such as `65.8 → 85.8`. Permit explicit acceptance of the independently verified source at the same visual location, or withhold the cell. Switching a whole page is inadequate when the sources fail in different cells. This costs human inspection and, only under `--spend`, additional OCR. [Comparison rule](DESIGN-FULL.md#L1970) · [Reading restriction](DESIGN-FULL.md#L1678) · [Dropped amounts and digit errors](dossiers/VAL.md#L35) · [Textract confidence guidance](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/textract/latest/dg/textract-best-practices.html)

**11. Estimates.** **Step 12 is my likeliest threefold overrun** in human review time. It combines six operating and three capital tables with 32 historical records to resolve. One operating statement runs about 430 rows. One capital subtotal has 223 contributors. For the capital prior-years statement, even the row grain and hierarchy were unknown when estimated. A short module can encode a mistaken interpretation of every one of those rows. Split acceptance by statement, and time the first difficult edition including Nick’s review before estimating the remaining eight. That delays the family-wide promise and produces a measured estimate. Step 4 is the earliest underestimate already visible in the launch corpus. There is no per-step labor estimate in the current plan against which to measure a literal factor of three. [Step 12](DESIGN-FULL.md#L4334) · [Operating statement](dossiers/SUPOP.md#L94) · [Capital evidence](dossiers/SUPCAP.md#L91)

**12. Held-out test.** An empty kit diff tests API stability. It is weak evidence about the cost or correctness of adding a family. The QSMR already caused two kit changes. Its vendor parser moves `VOID` and splits pages in desk code. A word straddling the proposed gutter satisfies neither half’s predicate and can disappear before the downstream stray-word check. Keep the diff as a recorded result, add word conservation across the helper itself, count helper and record work, and score a previously unseen edition on source-verified rows, captions and amounts. FY2025 Q1 is available from the District and lies outside the two design editions. This costs one blind implementation and review exercise and gives a meaningful basis for the PyPI decision. [Gutter split](DESIGN-FULL.md#L3101) · [Prior QSMR-driven changes](DESIGN-FULL.md#L3152) · [District FY2025 Q1 report](https://www.philasd.org/budget/wp-content/uploads/sites/96/2024/11/FY25-Q1-Quarterly-Report.pdf)

**13. What is missing.** Two deliverables matter for a civic publication. First, a record for each article calculation that pins the release, numerator, denominator, fund scope, time basis, source pages and reviewer. A reader can otherwise reproduce the dataset and still be unable to reproduce the article’s vacancy rate. Second, a tested correction path for a *previously published wrong value*. The design carries previous rows forward when re-extraction blocks. That is sensible for a newly broken parser, but it also keeps a known-bad figure live unless the operator can withdraw it, mark the affected release and update the article. The plan mentions withdrawn releases and notes that the page overlay lost its build step. Assign those pieces a January acceptance test: trace one article figure to its page, then rehearse correcting it. The cost is a modest editorial record and one release rehearsal. ## Where I am unsure

The new parser, release writer and workspace do not exist, so the gate and race findings describe consequences of the specified algorithms.
